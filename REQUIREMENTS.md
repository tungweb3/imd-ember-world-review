# EMBEO / GOLD rules to evaluate

1. Chain is Ethereum mainnet, chain ID 1. Canonical EMBEO is `0x2b82cdeb8477415d541799abb0cc48044c8ea5f3`, 18 decimals; exact fixed runtime and supply must be checked before enabling deployed contracts.
2. One share is 100,000 EMBEO, or `100000000000000000000000` atomic units. Stake/withdraw shares are positive whole integers. Every deposit creates a distinct position owned by the calling wallet.
3. A share held throughout UTC `[00:00, next 00:00)` earns one integer GOLD only after the complete finalized range is indexed and the wallet's settlement page finishes. No browser clock, display-name change, provisional log or rolling 24-hour calculation can credit GOLD.
4. Example: deposit at 2026-10-16 UTC noon; the first complete day is October 17 and the earliest entitlement is October 18 UTC 00:00, subject to chain finality, indexing and backlog. A deposit exactly at UTC midnight can qualify for that day.
5. Owners may withdraw principal at any time. A portion withdrawn earns no GOLD for that UTC day; remaining eligible shares and earlier credited GOLD are retained. Withdrawals at the next midnight do not remove the preceding completed day's entitlement. New deposits do not inherit an older deposit's eligibility.
6. GOLD is a nontransferable server ledger keyed by the verified wallet address. No player transfer, trading, cashout or conversion route is part of this release. The reward ratio is not an EMBEO/GOLD market price. An existing member-name or legacy cached economy balance is not this ledger and is not imported.
7. Staking principal remains in the separate immutable vault. Service revenue goes through the independent payment contract to immutable treasury `0x1C651928150DADDDA9C2C040a9D4901d862f8eC4`. No site-owner/trial-management role may transfer principal or mint/debit arbitrary GOLD.
8. Services must be explicitly listed in the server catalogue with separate exact integer GOLD and/or EMBEO prices. This candidate has no production catalogue or invented prices. A server order captures its price, currency and wallet; a browser-supplied amount is never accepted as the price.
9. Existing World SIWE is identity only. Each EMBEO operation requires explicit review, exact allowance where needed, explicit zero reset if a nonzero allowance is insufficient, and separate final transaction confirmation. No automatic account request, chain switching, unlimited allowance or resend is permitted.
10. Each GOLD debit requires a fresh short-lived canonical `personal_sign` message binding origin, wallet, order, service, amount, request, nonce and expiry. It must not resemble or authorize SIWE, EMBEO, Permit or an allowance. The server verifies EOA/ECDSA authority at the final write boundary; ERC-1271 identities are read-only for this debit flow.
11. Debit, immutable journal, consumed challenge, order completion and entitlement must be one atomic transaction. Concurrent spending cannot create negative GOLD, duplicate journal entries or free fulfillment. A successful exact EMBEO payment is reconciled from its finalized receipt even when mining occurs after the displayed quote expiry.
12. Finalized block headers must be contiguous and exact pinned-emitter event/receipt/contract/payer/receiver/amount consistent. Outer receipt sender/recipient may identify a forwarder, constructor or batch rather than the event actor. Unrelated LOG0/arbitrary receipt data must not block a valid matching event. Duplicate/replayed ingestion is idempotent; incomplete ranges cannot advance the trusted horizon. A changed stored finalized hash produces a durable halt. Staged busy-block rows do not affect positions, orders or GOLD until the entire block commits. The 10,000-event ceiling is an engineering bound whose exceedance stalls cursor progress, not a per-transaction gas guarantee.
13. Settlement uses persistent page progress, capped per invocation at 64 positions and 32 wallets across all processed days. A wallet carry is issued only when complete. Closed lots are excluded from later days without erasing earlier eligible intervals. The separate minute cron leaves the existing identity/presence budgets and fifteen-minute housekeeping behavior intact.
14. Unknown sends retain a persisted intent before the wallet prompt and survive reopening. Read-back/storage failure stops sending. An original hash can be reconciled read-only; no-hash clearing requires explicit player acknowledgement after checking wallet history. Late old callbacks cannot overwrite newer intents. Browser-data deletion, another device or a mistaken manual acknowledgement remains a stated limitation.
15. Contract-controlled accounts may hold positions and accrue GOLD, but the existing EOA-only signed-debit rule does not let them spend it. Default production configuration stays closed. Mainnet contract deployment, runtime/immutable readback, migration/backup, RPC/finality/capacity configuration, actual accepted Swarm coverage and findings disposition, release pin and postflight must precede real funds enablement. Preserve original services and evidence throughout.
