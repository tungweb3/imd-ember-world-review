# EMBEO / GOLD rules to evaluate

1. Chain is Ethereum mainnet, chain ID 1. Canonical EMBEO is `0x2b82cdeb8477415d541799abb0cc48044c8ea5f3`, 18 decimals; exact fixed runtime and supply must be checked before enabling deployed contracts.
2. One share is 100,000 EMBEO, or `100000000000000000000000` atomic units. Stake/withdraw shares are positive whole integers. Every deposit creates a distinct position owned by the calling wallet.
3. A share held throughout UTC `[00:00, next 00:00)` earns one integer GOLD only after the complete finalized range is indexed and the wallet's settlement page finishes. No browser clock, display-name change, provisional log or rolling 24-hour calculation can credit GOLD.
4. Example: deposit at 2026-10-16 UTC noon; the first complete day is October 17 and the earliest entitlement is October 18 UTC 00:00, subject to chain finality, indexing and backlog. A deposit exactly at UTC midnight can qualify for that day.
5. Owners may withdraw principal at any time. A portion withdrawn earns no GOLD for that UTC day; remaining eligible shares and earlier credited GOLD are retained. Withdrawals at the next midnight do not remove the preceding completed day's entitlement. New deposits do not inherit an older deposit's eligibility.
6. GOLD is a nontransferable server ledger keyed by the verified wallet address. No player transfer, trading, cashout or conversion route is part of this release. The reward ratio is not an EMBEO/GOLD market price. An existing member-name or legacy cached economy balance is not this ledger and is not imported.
7. Staking principal remains in the separate immutable vault. Service revenue goes through the independent payment contract to immutable treasury `0x1C651928150DADDDA9C2C040a9D4901d862f8eC4`. No site-owner/trial-management role may transfer principal or mint/debit arbitrary GOLD.
8. Services must be explicitly listed in the server catalogue with separate exact integer GOLD and/or EMBEO prices. This candidate has no production catalogue or invented prices. A server order captures its price, currency and wallet; a browser-supplied amount is never accepted as the price.
9. Existing World SIWE is identity only. Each EMBEO operation requires explicit review, exact allowance where needed, explicit zero reset if a nonzero allowance is insufficient, and separate final transaction confirmation. No automatic account request, chain switching, unlimited allowance or resend is permitted.
10. Each GOLD debit requires a fresh short-lived canonical `personal_sign` message binding origin, wallet, order, service, amount, request, nonce and expiry. It must not resemble or authorize SIWE, EMBEO, Permit or an allowance. The server verifies EOA/ECDSA authority at the final write boundary; ERC-1271 identities are read-only for this debit flow.
11. Debit, immutable journal, consumed challenge, order completion and entitlement must be one atomic transaction. Concurrent spending cannot create negative GOLD, duplicate journal entries or free fulfillment. Orders use opaque server-generated 256-bit identifiers while wallet/request idempotence preserves the winning immutable quote; the ID alone is not payment authorization. A successful exact EMBEO payment is reconciled from its finalized receipt even after displayed quote expiry or when a same-slot whole-second block timestamp precedes server-created milliseconds. Creation of quotes/challenges must use fresh time after reads and final SQL session/revocation/expiry guards.
12. Finalized block headers must be contiguous and exact pinned-emitter event/receipt/contract/payer/receiver/amount consistent. Any obtained numeric header at an announced finalized height must match that complete finalized identity and the optional witness. Bounded pages below it still trust RPC ancestry and complete log enumeration. Outer receipt sender/recipient may identify a forwarder, constructor or batch rather than the event actor. Unrelated LOG0/arbitrary receipt data must not block a valid matching event. Duplicate/replayed ingestion is idempotent; incomplete ranges cannot advance the trusted horizon. A changed stored finalized hash produces a durable halt. Staged busy-block rows do not affect positions, orders or GOLD until the entire block commits. The 10,000-event ceiling is an engineering bound whose exceedance stalls cursor progress, not a per-transaction gas guarantee.
13. Settlement uses persistent page progress, capped per invocation at 64 positions and 32 wallets across all processed days. A wallet carry is issued only when complete. Closed lots are excluded from later days without erasing earlier eligible intervals. The separate minute cron leaves the existing identity/presence budgets and fifteen-minute housekeeping behavior intact.
14. Unknown sends retain a persisted chain/nonce-bound intent before the wallet prompt and survive reopening. Read-back/storage failure stops sending. Same-origin Web Locks cover the complete send/prompt boundary; unavailable or busy locks fail closed without a queued send. Original wallet-returned hashes use ordinary mined confirmation; manually supplied replacement hashes require the same sender/chain/nonce and canonical finalized inclusion. A different payload is a replacement, not successful execution of the original plan. Wrong candidates cannot change saved intent, and adoption clears unrelated plans. Legacy/hashless clearing requires explicit player acknowledgement after checking wallet history. Late old callbacks cannot overwrite newer intents. Browser-data deletion, other profiles/devices/origins/wallet actions and mistaken manual acknowledgement remain stated limitations, not proof of non-broadcast.
15. Contract-controlled accounts may hold positions and accrue GOLD, but the existing EOA-only signed-debit rule does not let them spend it. Default production configuration stays closed. Mainnet contract deployment, runtime/immutable readback, migration/backup, RPC/finality/capacity configuration, actual accepted Swarm coverage and findings disposition, release pin and postflight must precede real funds enablement. Preserve original services and evidence throughout.

16. Validate actual server-to-client paid snapshots across the timestamp domains; require strict JSON enum types. Recheck expiry and chain halt after pending-nonce awaits before the wallet send boundary. GOLD must recheck an observed halt after the final authorization await and before POST, but a later halt after a completed payment POST must not erase its valid paid result. Malformed request types return a bounded client error with no writes. The deployment readback preflight compares complete finalized/numeric header identity and cross-provider identity, including parent/time/gas when available.

17. Defend against hypothetical server-clock regression across requests/workers with persisted lower bounds: GOLD challenge issuance must be at or after order.createdMs, and debit time must be at or after order.createdMs and challenge.issuedMs. Reject before any write, do not clamp time. This is defensive server behavior, not evidence of a client-controlled clock or confirmed production incident. Valid EMBEO same-slot chain/server timestamp skew remains legal.

18. A staking domain (chain ID, canonical token, vault) is durably bound to one config ID. Catalogue-only changes keep that ID. Repinning deploymentBlock/paymentContract must fail closed before a second historical reward; concurrent first binding and existing single-config partial days must remain safe. Ambiguous legacy history is preserved and blocked, never silently rewritten. An intentional repin requires a separately reviewed explicit migration.
19. A matching completed GOLD payment may be replayed after challenge and quote expiry only after original identity, signature, request and persisted time checks. Return paid without a new debit/journal/entitlement. Fresh unpaid expired challenges must still fail.
20. Payment replay is keyed by payer/order/exact amount. A wrong manual amount cannot consume the correct amount slot; exact tuple replay is rejected. The client reads paid(address,bytes32,uint256). Wrong manual transfers remain irreversible treasury transfers with no entitlement or automatic refund; no rescue/admin privilege is introduced.
21. Pending expired order replay returns ORDER_EXPIRED. Explicit player renewal uses a new request ID, while ordinary network retry retains its original ID. Historical paid replay and late exact finalized EMBEO reconciliation remain valid.

Treat this new scope as needing independent review, not as covered by the old accepted Audit or the previous b46 local tests.

Review the independent event-derived UTC entitlement model, comparison against EARNED journals plus prior adjustments (never spendable balance), positive-only corrections, overissuance refusal, exact retained old event proofs, same finalized boundary, settled old/new days, all-financial-table SQL snapshot guard, atomic receipt/binding/lineage/import/day closure/adjustments and exact-hash replay. Exercise concurrent spend/indexer changes, rollback/retry, second historical rebuild and another legitimate vault. Normal settlement must resume after the recovered horizon and never reissue historical days. Retired configs must fail final-write/indexer/page guards; independent onchain principal withdrawal remains unchanged.

The controlled rebuilt input must originate from the existing finalized receipt-verifying indexer, not a player event upload. Local copies are not independent chain consensus proof. The internal module and offline SQLite tool add no HTTP route, privileged wallet, arbitrary credit parameter, RPC credential, remote D1 command, refund or automatic deployment. Review read-only input hashes and fresh side-by-side output, the private migration batch and the explicit reviewed-plan boundary. A correct later exact payment fulfills once and stays linked to the original exception, not falsely marked refunded. Legacy evidence is rebuilt256/page with bounded operator-copy tooling.
